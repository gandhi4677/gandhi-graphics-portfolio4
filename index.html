01 / ABOUT ME

A little about
my creativity.

I am Gandhi, and I have a strong interest in graphic design. I enjoy turning ideas into attractive visual designs. I like experimenting with colours, typography, layouts and creative elements to make every design look clear, memorable and meaningful.

My design interests include invitation cards for special occasions, business branding, promotional banners and social media creatives. I focus on understanding the purpose of each design and creating a layout that suits the occasion or brand.

I’m continuously learning, practising and improving my design skills. I’m excited to connect with people who need creative design support.
